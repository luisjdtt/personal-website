About Me

Hi, I'm Luis

I'm a Technical Lead and Software Architect from Mexico. I build production systems across AWS serverless, full-stack web, mobile, backend architecture, enterprise integrations, and AI-agent platforms.

My strongest work is Roddy, a multi-tenant B2B AI-agent platform used by real clients to automate sales and operational workflows across WhatsApp, email, webchat, ERPs, CRMs, and internal systems. I designed and built the core backend, frontend, middleware, authentication, integrations, AWS deployment, documentation, and production configuration.

I also work on React Native mobile apps, Django backends, offline-first workflows, geolocation, synchronization, ERP/CRM integrations, WebSockets, SSO, computer vision, recommendation systems, and LLM tool orchestration.

I care about pragmatic architecture: systems that can be understood, operated, secured, and evolved by real teams under real constraints.
